Keyboard shortcuts

Press Tab to get into the binder, then:

← ↑ ↓ →
Move from pocket to pocket (past the left or right edge turns the page)
PgUp PgDn
Turn the page back / forward
Enter
Open the card in the MTG Card Finder (or add a card to an empty pocket)
Space
Pick up the card. Then the arrows choose a pocket, Space drops it, Esc cancels
+ −
One more / one fewer copy
Delete
Remove the card (it asks first)
M
Move the card to another binder
